DatePicker: unselectable days are not exposed as disabled to assistive technology

The 15th is in disabledDates, and the days of the adjacent months are not selectable either. Neither their <td> (the gridcell) nor the day <span> carries aria-disabled. The only signal is the p-disabled class, which a screen reader does not see. [pt] cannot add it conditionally either, because ptm('dayCell') receives no per-date context.
